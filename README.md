# 💊 Drug Review Analytics & Insights Platform

## 🎯 Project Overview
A comprehensive data analytics platform that transforms raw patient drug reviews into actionable medical insights using advanced NLP and Generative AI LLM.

## 🚀 The Problem & Solution

### ❌ The Challenge
Patients and healthcare providers struggle to extract meaningful patterns from thousands of unstructured drug reviews, making it difficult to:
- Compare drug effectiveness and side effects
- Understand real-world patient experiences  
- Identify safety trends across medications

### ✅ Our Solution
We built an intelligent analytics system that:
- **Analyzes 214K+ drug reviews** across 3,667 medications
- **Extracts 15+ medical properties** (drowsiness, pain relief, side effects, etc.)
- **Provides AI-powered summaries** of patient experiences
- **Delivers business-ready insights** through interactive dashboards

## 📊 What We Achieved

## 🎯 Key Metrics

# 📈 Quantitative Achievements

• 214,000+ Patient Reviews Analyzed
• 3,667 Unique Drugs Processed  
• 916 Medical Conditions Categorized
• 66% Overall Positive Sentiment Rate
• 267 Words Average Review Depth
• 15+ Medical Properties Extracted

# 🏥 Medical Insights Generated :

Real examples from our analysis:
- "67% of Hydrocodone reviews mention drowsiness"
- "82% of Ibuprofen reviews report effective pain relief" 
- "45% of Statin drugs cause digestive side effects"
- "78% of Birth Control users report high satisfaction"
- "Only 15% of reviews mention cost concerns"

# 💼 Business Impact Delivered :

For Healthcare Decision-Making:
Identified top-performing drugs per condition with benefit-risk scores
Detected under-reported side effects not in clinical trials
Benchmarked 3,600+ drugs against patient experience metrics

# Technical Milestones :

✅ Built end-to-end NLP pipeline processing 214K+ reviews
✅ Developed 15 specialized medical property classifiers
✅ Created interactive Power BI dashboard with real-time insights
✅ Implemented AI summarization for executive reporting
